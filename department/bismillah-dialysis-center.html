About Bismillah Dialysis Center

Located in the heart of Hajigonj, Chandpur, within Bismillah General Hospital, our dialysis center is equipped with state-of-the-art technology and follows international standards.

  • ISO 9001:2015 certified water treatment plant
  • Individual patient monitoring with central console
  • Hepatitis B/C negative zone with strict protocols
  • In-house pharmacy, lab, and ICU support
  • Corporate & insurance partnerships
