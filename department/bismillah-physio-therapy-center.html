When most people think of a Physiotherapist they imagine somebody running onto the sports field with a bucket & sponge. While the treatment of sports injuries is one aspect of our profession, Physiotherapy has evolved beyond this & is now acknowledged as an essential element in the management of most musculoskeletal, neurological & rheumatologic conditions.
